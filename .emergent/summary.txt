Users face issues with opportunity stage progression, data inheritance from leads, missing approval workflows, and an incomplete master data management system.

**Detailed Requirements:**
1.  **Opportunity Stage Management**: Opportunities list must reflect the current stage (e.g., L2 - Qualification). Manage Stages form must open directly to the current stage. The stage dropdown should be removed from the opportunities edit form.
